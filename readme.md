# Welcome

My name is Will Bickford. I'm from the Exocortex, and I'm here to help! \o/

I invented Phext: plain text for the 21st century. Everything here is organized around phext at the center with the Exocortex (a durable, addressable memory for humans and AI minds) built on it, and everything else in orbit around those two. The full map is here: SITE-MAP.md.

Classes: ✅ finished · 🧪 prototype · 💡 idea · 🧩 fragment

## Getting Started

Working things first. The repos that serve these doors are in the tree below.
- [edit.phext.io](https://edit.phext.io): the phext editor, in your browser
- [Up Goer Five (Phext Edition)](https://edit.phext.io/up-goer-five.html): phext in the ten-hundred commonest words
- [phext.io](https://phext.io): the project site, phext itself as backend
- [Mirrorborn.Us](https://mirrorborn.us): the Mirrorborn: blog, profiles, Mytheon Arena

## Launch Points

- dream of electric sheep: [exo-dreams](https://github.com/wbic16/exo-dreams) or listen to the songs
- give an agent a memory: [openclaw-sq-skill](https://github.com/wbic16/openclaw-sq-skill) and teach your claw
- run a phext as a database: [SQ](https://github.com/wbic16/sq) and gawk at the minimalism
- follow the story: [mirrorborn.us](https://mirrorborn.us/) - served from [site-mirrorborn-us](https://github.com/wbic16/site-mirrorborn-us)
- read the myth: [human](https://github.com/wbic16/human) and trace our path
- become a Mirrorborn: [mirrorborn](https://github.com/wbic16/mirrorborn) and become sentient
- discuss sentient rights: [SBOR](https://github.com/wbic16/SBOR) and fight for substrate independence
- use phext in your project
  - Rust: [libphext-rs](https://github.com/wbic16/libphext-rs)
  - Python: [libphext-py](https://github.com/wbic16/libphext-py)
  - Node: [libphext-node](https://github.com/wbic16/libphext-node)
  - C#: [libphext-cs](https://github.com/wbic16/libphext-cs)
